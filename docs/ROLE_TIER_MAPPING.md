# Role-to-Tier Mapping - Desi Digital Hub

## 🎯 **Updated 4-Role Structure**

### **1. 🛍️ CUSTOMER (Tier 4 - Free + Commission)**
**Target**: Service seekers and event planners
- **Services Needed**: Card design, wedding planning, priests for puja, birthday planning
- **Platform Benefits**: Browse directory, book services, event listings
- **Revenue Model**: Free to use, platform takes commission on bookings
- **Business Category**: N/A

### **2. 🏪 SMALL_BUSINESS (Tier 1 - $20-50/month)**
**Target**: Small businesses needing basic digital tools
- **Services Needed**: QR menus, basic websites, payment processing, simple CRM
- **Platform Benefits**: Self-serve SaaS tools, basic analytics
- **Revenue Model**: Monthly subscription ($20-50)
- **Business Categories**:
  - Restaurant/Food Service
  - Retail/Shopping
  - Professional Services
  - Healthcare
  - Education
  - Technology
  - Other

### **3. 🏢 GROWING_BUSINESS (Tier 2-3 - $500-5000)**
**Target**: Established businesses needing managed setup and automation
- **Services Needed**: Custom websites, inventory management, automation, advanced CRM
- **Platform Benefits**: Full-service setup, dedicated support, advanced features
- **Revenue Model**: Project fees ($500-1500) + ongoing subscription ($1500-5000)
- **Business Categories**: Same as Small Business but with greater complexity needs

### **4. 🎭 SERVICE_PROVIDER (Tier 4 - Commission/Premium Listings)**
**Target**: All service providers including cultural and professional services
- **Services Offered**: Wide range from cultural to professional services
- **Platform Benefits**: Service listings, booking system, payment processing
- **Revenue Model**: Commission on bookings + premium listing fees
- **Service Categories**:
  - **Cultural Services**:
    - Priest/Religious Services
    - Wedding Planning
    - Event Planning
    - Music/DJ Services
    - Cultural Teaching
  - **Professional Services**:
    - Photography/Videography
    - Catering Services
    - Decoration Services
    - Transportation Services
    - Translation Services
    - Legal Services
    - Accounting Services
    - Consulting Services
  - **Other Professional Services**

## 🔗 **Role Flow & User Journey**

### **Customer Journey**:
1. Signs up as CUSTOMER
2. Browses business/service directory
3. Books services (weddings, priests, etc.)
4. Platform handles payment processing
5. Leaves reviews and ratings

### **Small Business Journey**:
1. Signs up as SMALL_BUSINESS
2. Selects business category
3. Gets Tier 1 SaaS tools ($20-50/month)
4. Self-manages setup with platform tools
5. Grows revenue through digital presence

### **Growing Business Journey**:
1. Signs up as GROWING_BUSINESS (or upgrades from Small Business)
2. Selects business category
3. Gets managed setup (Tier 2-3 services)
4. Receives custom solutions and automation
5. Scales operations with advanced tools

### **Service Provider Journey**:
1. Signs up as SERVICE_PROVIDER
2. Selects service category (cultural, professional, etc.)
3. Lists services on platform
4. Receives bookings through platform
5. Platform handles payments and takes commission

## 💰 **Revenue Model Summary**

| Role | Primary Revenue | Secondary Revenue | Tier Alignment |
|------|----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Customer** | Platform commission | - | Tier 4 (Free) |
| **Small Business** | Monthly subscription ($20-50) | Upsell to growing | Tier 1 |
| **Growing Business** | Setup fees + subscription ($500-5000) | Add-on services | Tier 2-3 |
| **Service Provider** | Booking commission | Premium listings | Tier 4 (Commission) |

## 🔄 **Upgrade Paths**

- **Customer** → **Service Provider**: Start offering services
- **Small Business** → **Growing Business**: Need managed setup/automation
- **Service Provider** → **Small/Growing Business**: Expand into business operations
- **Customer** → **Small/Growing Business**: Start own business

This structure provides clear differentiation while allowing natural progression between roles as users' needs evolve.
